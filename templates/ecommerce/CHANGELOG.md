# Template Ecommerce — changelog

A new template version arrives here as instructions for your AI to apply to
your copy (G6). Nothing ever overwrites the code you downloaded.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-28

- Store from the Infi storefront, prices computed by the server.
- Checkout in the Infi frame (`@beinfi/checkout`): pix and card; card fields in the provider's frame.
- Digital delivery: the file in `assets/` is uploaded by `infi sync` and mailed by Infi after payment.
- Monthly subscription with cancel at period end.
- "Minhas compras" (`PortalElement`): orders with receipt, downloads, subscriptions, refund within the guarantee.
